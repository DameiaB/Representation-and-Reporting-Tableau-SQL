# 🏥 Healthcare Analysis Dashboard

Welcome to the **Healthcare Analysis Dashboard**!  
This Power BI project offers an in-depth exploration of healthcare tool usage across various dimensions, including diagnoses, genders, races, and patient demographics. The interactive dashboard provides insights into the distribution of diagnoses and highlights key profitability metrics to support data-driven decision-making.

## 📊 Project Overview

This dashboard evaluates a dataset of patients and the outcomes of their initial tool usage. These metrics can help monitor the tool’s effectiveness in achieving its purpose of improving equitable clinical trial screening and supporting research coordinators in identifying potential candidates. It is created to provide coordinators, analysts, and providers with a comprehensive overview.


### Key Questions Addressed:
- How many patients used the tool compared to those who did not?
- What is the most common disease among patients, and which demographic group is most prevalent?
- Which diagnosis had the highest overall number of patients?

---

## 💡 Key Metrics

- **Total Patients who used the tool**: 5,200  
- **Most Prominent Demographic**: White  
- **Most Common Disease**: MASH (49.28%)  
- **Percentage of Patients with Fibrosis**: 63.37%

---

### ⭐ Symptom Distribution
- **Ascites** – 671 patients
- **Encephalopathy** – 379 patients
- **Variceal Bleeding** – 138 patients
> Ascites lead in symptoms reported by a patient.
